**ffex** is a complete digital-asset exchange platform solution: spot,
perpetual futures, options and OTC trading, wallet custody, risk and
clearing, notifications, back-office, and the admin / H5 / mobile frontends
— delivered as dual Java and Go implementations of one contract-first
blueprint.

This blog publishes engineering articles about how the platform is built to
behave: domain boundaries, throughput design, fund-safety discipline,
observability and operations. It is written for operators and engineers
evaluating exchange infrastructure.

What you will not find here is the internals — matching core data
structures, capacity engineering, tuning playbooks. Those are shared with
partners under NDA.
